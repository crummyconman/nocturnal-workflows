Common questions

Who are these kits for?

Instant Lead Response is for real estate agents who want a first reply to go out when a new web lead comes in. Invoice Reminders + Quote Follow-Up is for freelancers and tradespeople who send invoices or quotes from a spreadsheet, Word or Gmail. If you already use QuickBooks, FreshBooks or Stripe invoicing, those tools have their own reminders, so you probably don't need that kit.

Do I have to give you my email to download a kit?

No. It's a direct download, no email required. No ads and no card needed.

How do I tell the test worked?

For Invoice Reminders + Quote Follow-Up, add a test row with an alias of your own email (like you+test@gmail.com). The test reminder or quote check-in arrives at that alias, and the sheet logs the date it was sent. Test mode only emails your own addresses, so nothing reaches a real customer until you switch it off. For Instant Lead Response, send a test lead and check the reply before going live. Use made-up test leads.

What happens if I ask you to install it?

We do the install with you on a screen-share call. You stay signed in to your own accounts and click the permission screens yourself. You pay the install fee only after the test run works on the call, and there's no charge if the install can't be finished. After install, the emails still go out from your Gmail under your name, so you remain the sender.

Does this site see my customers or leads?

No. This site doesn't collect your clients' or leads' information. If you email us or book a call, we get the name and email you give us, and we use them only to reply. If you sign up for new free workflows, we get the email and first name you give us, through Google Forms, and use them only to send those updates. You can unsubscribe anytime. If you run a kit, your data goes through the services you connect, under their terms. Read the privacy note.
