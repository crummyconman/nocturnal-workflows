What's in the setup guide

  1. What you need: the accounts the workflow uses and which plans fit your lead volume. Free AI plans may use what you send through them, so check the terms before running real client data through one.
  2. Connect your lead form: send new form entries into the workflow.
  3. Write your reply: set the AI's instructions, your name, and your tone.
  4. Get alerts: receive a notice when a lead comes in and the reply goes out.
  5. Texting (optional): add text replies once consent and carrier registration are in place.
  6. Test it: send a test lead and check the reply before going live.
  7. Stay compliant: the checklist in the section below.

Before you use it

  • Texting consent: only text leads who agreed to receive automated texts from you on your form. Honor STOP and other opt-out requests right away.
  • Fair Housing: replies must never steer or treat anyone differently based on race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, or any protection under your state's law. Review your reply instructions and keep a record of what's sent.
  • Your data: check each service's terms on how it handles the lead information you send through it.
  • Not legal advice: check your state's rules and your brokerage's policies before turning it on.
