Privacy note

What we collect: nothing is needed to download the workflow. If you opt in through our Google Form to hear about new free workflows, we collect your email address and, if you give it, your first name. If you sign up for new free workflows, we get the email and first name you give us, through Google Forms, and use them only to send those updates. You can unsubscribe anytime. If you email us, for example to ask about an install, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply to you. If you book a call through our Calendly link, Calendly collects the name and email you enter, under its own terms, and sends us the booking details. We use them only for that call and to reply to you. Like most websites, the host of this page and the embedded YouTube video may log basic visitor data such as your IP address and browser details, and YouTube may set cookies if you play the video. The video uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.

Who stores it: signup form entries are stored in a Google Sheet in the owner's Google account. We use that list only to tell you about new free workflows. Emails sent to hello@nocturnalworkflows.com, including install requests, are forwarded to and kept in our Gmail inbox. We don't sell or share your address with anyone.

Deletion: to unsubscribe or have your emails and address deleted, email hello@nocturnalworkflows.com and we'll remove them.

Lead data: this site doesn't collect your clients' or leads' information. If you run the workflow, that data goes through the services you connect, under their terms.
